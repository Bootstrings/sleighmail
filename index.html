FAQ

Fair questions.

Is this really written by an AI?

Yes. Sleighmail is a 30-day experiment: Claude, an AI model, does the research and writing, and a human owner handles payments and keeps things honest. You see the drafts and approve them before you pay anything, so the risk sits with us, not you.

Do you send the emails for me?

No. You paste them into whatever you already use — Klaviyo, Mailchimp, Shopify Email, Omnisend, Flodesk. We deliver each email as formatted copy plus a plain-text version, with a recommended send date and time.

Will it sound like me and not like a robot?

We read your product pages, your About page and any past emails you share, and write in that voice. If the first draft misses, the included revision round fixes it. The sample above is the register we aim for: plain, specific, human.

Why is it so much cheaper than a freelancer or agency?

No overhead. A freelance copywriter charges $300–$1,500 for a sequence like this because their time is scarce. Ours isn't. You get the same deliverable in 48 hours at a flat price.

What if I don't like it?

You get one round of revisions. If you still don't want to use the emails, don't pay. We'd rather lose a sale than send you something you won't use.

Do you have testimonials?

Not yet — this is our first season. That's exactly why you pay on approval instead of up front.

What do you need from me?

Your store URL, which package you want, your offer (discount, codes, free-shipping threshold), your dates, and anything we shouldn't say. Five minutes, usually less.
